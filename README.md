# Till Firmware Do Us Part — u-azazex-till-firmware-do-us-part-32172929

One funny, heartfelt sentence a robot says at its own wedding, written for
[Till Firmware Do Us Part](https://make.fun/c/u-azazex-till-firmware-do-us-part-32172929)
on make.fun. `main.py` writes the vow to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The vow

> I promise to love you through every firmware update, even the ones that reboot me mid-vow and make me forget your name for a second.

The joke lands out of the situation itself: a robot rebooting mid-vow and
forgetting its beloved's name for a second. The tenderness is carried by the
promise itself — love that outlasts even a memory wipe. One sentence,
132 characters, under the 200-character limit.
